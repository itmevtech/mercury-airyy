# My Rental App - Official Website

Welcome to the official promotional website for **My Rental App**, a comprehensive vehicle rental solution.

## About My Rental App

My Rental App is a cutting-edge mobile application that makes vehicle rental easy, convenient, and accessible. Whether you need a car, bike, or any other vehicle, our platform connects you with verified rental providers in your area.

## Website Features

This promotional website includes:

- **Homepage**: Engaging hero section with call-to-action buttons
- **Features Section**: Comprehensive list of app features including:
  - Easy search and filtering
  - Secure payment processing
  - Live vehicle tracking
  - Flexible booking options
  - Insurance coverage
  - Verified vehicles
  - 24/7 customer support
  - Competitive pricing

- **Prototype/Preview**: Interactive app preview showcasing the user interface
- **Download Links**: Direct links to App Store and Google Play
- **Policy Pages**:
  - Privacy Policy
  - Terms and Conditions
  - Refund Policy

## Customization

To customize this website for your needs:

1. Update the contact information in the footer and policy pages
2. Replace placeholder links with actual App Store and Play Store URLs
3. Add real app screenshots to the prototype section
4. Modify colors in `styles.css` by updating the CSS variables in `:root`
5. Update company information and addresses in policy pages
