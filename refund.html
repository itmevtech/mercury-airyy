Refund Policy

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

At My Rental App, we strive to provide the best vehicle rental experience. This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions for cancellations and refunds. Please read this policy carefully before making a booking.

1. Cancellation by Renter

1.1 Cancellation Timeframes and Refunds

The refund amount depends on when you cancel your booking relative to the scheduled pickup time:

More than 48 Hours Before Pickup

  • Refund: Full refund of the rental amount (100%)
  • Processing Fee: No cancellation fee
  • Service Fee: Service fees are refunded
  • Processing Time: 5-7 business days

24-48 Hours Before Pickup

  • Refund: 50% of the rental amount
  • Processing Fee: Standard processing fee applies
  • Service Fee: Service fees are non-refundable
  • Processing Time: 5-7 business days

Less than 24 Hours Before Pickup

  • Refund: No refund
  • Processing Fee: Not applicable
  • Service Fee: Non-refundable
  • Exception: May be eligible for partial refund in case of documented emergencies (see Section 1.3)

No-Show (Failure to Pick Up Vehicle)

  • Refund: No refund
  • Additional Charges: May apply as per the rental agreement

1.2 How to Cancel

To cancel a booking:

  • Open the My Rental App and navigate to "My Bookings"
  • Select the booking you wish to cancel
  • Tap "Cancel Booking" and follow the prompts
  • You will receive a cancellation confirmation via email and in-app notification
  • Refunds (if applicable) will be processed to the original payment method

1.3 Emergency Cancellations

In case of documented emergencies, we may provide exceptions to our standard cancellation policy. Eligible emergencies include:

  • Medical emergencies (requires medical documentation)
  • Death in the immediate family (requires death certificate or obituary)
  • Natural disasters or severe weather conditions
  • Government-imposed travel restrictions
  • Sudden military deployment (requires official orders)

To request an emergency cancellation refund, contact our support team at support@myrentalapp.com with relevant documentation within 48 hours of the scheduled pickup time.

2. Cancellation by Vehicle Owner or My Rental App

2.1 Owner Cancellation

If the vehicle owner cancels your confirmed booking:

  • Refund: Full refund (100%) of all amounts paid
  • Processing Time: 3-5 business days
  • Alternative: We will assist you in finding a comparable replacement vehicle
  • Compensation: You may receive a credit voucher for future use

2.2 Cancellation Due to Vehicle Issues

If the vehicle becomes unavailable due to mechanical issues, damage, or safety concerns:

  • Full refund of all amounts paid
  • We will offer alternative vehicles at the same or lower price
  • If no suitable alternative is available, you may receive a credit for future bookings

2.3 Cancellation for Policy Violations

If we cancel your booking due to:

  • Providing false information
  • Failed verification checks
  • Previous violations of our Terms and Conditions
  • Suspected fraudulent activity

Refund: No refund will be provided

3. Refund Eligibility

3.1 Eligible for Refund

  • Cancellations made within the eligible timeframe
  • Vehicle unavailability due to owner or platform issues
  • Significant discrepancies between vehicle description and actual condition
  • Documented emergencies (subject to verification)
  • Technical errors resulting in duplicate charges

3.2 Not Eligible for Refund

  • Changes in personal plans or preferences
  • Weather conditions that do not constitute emergencies
  • Late cancellations (less than 24 hours before pickup)
  • No-shows without valid emergency documentation
  • Voluntary early returns (unless specified in rental agreement)
  • Dissatisfaction with vehicle aesthetics (minor cosmetic issues)

4. Partial Refunds

4.1 Shortened Rental Period

If you return the vehicle earlier than the agreed return time:

  • Standard Policy: No refund for unused rental time
  • Exception: If the vehicle has significant issues that prevent use, you may be eligible for a pro-rated refund (requires immediate reporting and documentation)

4.2 Service Interruptions

If you experience significant service interruptions during your rental:

  • Vehicle breakdown: Pro-rated refund for downtime exceeding 2 hours
  • Misrepresented vehicle features: Up to 25% refund depending on severity
  • Safety issues requiring early return: Full refund for unused portion

4.3 Modifications to Booking

If you modify your booking (dates, times, vehicle type):

  • No refund for the difference if switching to a less expensive vehicle
  • Additional payment required if switching to a more expensive vehicle
  • Modification fees may apply depending on timing

5. Processing Time

5.1 Standard Refund Timeline

  • Credit/Debit Cards: 5-10 business days from cancellation
  • Digital Wallets: 3-7 business days from cancellation
  • Bank Transfers: 7-14 business days from cancellation

5.2 Security Deposit Refund

  • Timeline: 7-14 business days after rental completion
  • Conditions: Subject to vehicle inspection and verification of no damage
  • Deductions: May be deducted for damages, cleaning, late fees, or other charges

5.3 Delays

Refund processing may be delayed due to:

  • Bank processing times
  • International transactions
  • Ongoing dispute investigations
  • Verification requirements
  • Holidays and weekends

6. Non-Refundable Items

The following items are non-refundable in all circumstances:

  • Service Fees: Platform service fees (except in cases of owner cancellation or platform error)
  • Processing Fees: Payment processing fees
  • Optional Add-ons: Once the rental period begins (GPS, child seats, etc.)
  • Insurance Premiums: After the rental period starts
  • Promotional Credits: Used promotional credits or discount codes
  • Third-Party Fees: Tolls, parking, traffic violations, or fuel charges

7. Refund Method

Refunds are processed using the same payment method used for the original transaction. We do not provide refunds in cash or alternative payment methods. If the original payment method is no longer valid:

  • Contact our support team immediately
  • Provide alternative payment details
  • Additional verification may be required
  • Processing time may be extended

8. Disputes and Claims

8.1 Filing a Dispute

If you disagree with a refund decision or amount:

  • Contact our support team at support@myrentalapp.com
  • Provide your booking reference number and detailed explanation
  • Include any supporting documentation
  • We will review and respond within 5-7 business days

8.2 Escalation Process

If the initial dispute resolution is unsatisfactory:

  • Request escalation to our disputes team
  • Provide additional evidence if available
  • Final decision will be communicated within 10-14 business days
  • Decisions made by the disputes team are final

8.3 Chargeback Policy

Before initiating a chargeback with your bank:

  • Contact our support team to resolve the issue
  • Chargebacks without prior contact may result in account suspension
  • We reserve the right to dispute unwarranted chargebacks
  • Account privileges may be restricted pending chargeback resolution

9. Special Circumstances

9.1 Long-Term Rentals

For rentals exceeding 7 days:

  • Custom cancellation terms may apply
  • Pro-rated refunds may be available for qualifying cancellations
  • Refer to your specific rental agreement for details

9.2 Corporate Bookings

Corporate accounts may have different refund policies as specified in their agreement with My Rental App.

9.3 Subscription Plans

If you're enrolled in a subscription or membership plan:

  • Subscription fees are billed separately and have their own refund policy
  • Individual rentals within a subscription follow this refund policy
  • Contact support for subscription-specific cancellations

10. Policy Changes

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting in the app with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the modified policy. However, any bookings made before policy changes will be subject to the policy in effect at the time of booking.

For questions about refunds, cancellations, or to request a refund:

  • Customer Support: support@myrentalapp.com
  • Phone: +1 (555) 123-4567

  • Emergency Support: Available 24/7 for active rentals

12. Important Notes

  • All refund amounts are in the currency of the original transaction
  • Currency conversion fees (if applicable) are non-refundable
  • Keep your booking confirmation and cancellation emails for records
  • Screenshots of cancellation confirmations can help expedite disputes
  • This Refund Policy should be read in conjunction with our Terms and Conditions
