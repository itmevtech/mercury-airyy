Refund Policy

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

1. Overview

At My Rental App, we strive to provide excellent service and customer satisfaction. This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which refunds may be issued for vehicle rental bookings made through our platform.

2. Cancellation and Refund Schedule

The refund amount depends on when you cancel your booking relative to the scheduled pickup time:

Cancellation Timeframe Refund Amount
More than 48 hours before pickup 100% refund
24-48 hours before pickup 50% refund
12-24 hours before pickup 25% refund
Less than 12 hours before pickup No refund
No-show (failure to pick up vehicle) No refund

3. Full Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund regardless of the cancellation timeframe if:

  • The vehicle is not available at the scheduled pickup time
  • The vehicle does not match the description or photos in the listing
  • The vehicle has significant mechanical issues or safety concerns
  • We cancel the booking due to reasons within our control
  • There is a documented emergency (medical, family, natural disaster) with proper verification

4. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in the following situations:

  • Early Return: If you return the vehicle early, you may be eligible for a partial refund of unused days (minimum 24 hours of unused time required)
  • Service Issues: If there are significant service issues during your rental that we cannot resolve promptly
  • Vehicle Issues: If the vehicle develops problems during the rental period that significantly impact your use

5. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Change of plans or personal circumstances (unless covered by emergency provisions)
  • Weather conditions or natural events beyond our control
  • Issues caused by renter negligence or misuse
  • Minor inconveniences that do not significantly impact the rental
  • Traffic violations, accidents, or damages during your rental period
  • Bookings cancelled after the rental period has begun (except for valid service issues)

6. Modification Fees

You may modify your booking without a fee if done more than 24 hours before the scheduled pickup time. Modifications made within 24 hours of pickup may incur a $25 administrative fee.

7. Refund Processing

7.1 Processing Time: Approved refunds will be processed within 5-10 business days of approval.

7.2 Refund Method: Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the booking.

7.3 Notification: You will receive an email notification when your refund has been processed.

8. Damage and Cleaning Fees

The following fees are non-refundable and will be deducted from any applicable refund:

  • Damage Fees: Charges for any damage to the vehicle beyond normal wear and tear
  • Cleaning Fees: Charges if the vehicle is returned excessively dirty or with odors (smoking, pets, etc.)
  • Late Return Fees: Charges for returning the vehicle later than the scheduled time
  • Fuel Fees: Charges if the vehicle is not returned with the same fuel level

9. Dispute Resolution

If you disagree with a refund decision:

  • Contact our customer support team within 14 days of the decision
  • Provide all relevant documentation and evidence
  • We will review your case and respond within 5 business days
  • If the dispute remains unresolved, it may be escalated to our management team

10. Special Circumstances

10.1 Force Majeure: In cases of extraordinary events (natural disasters, pandemics, government restrictions), special refund provisions may apply. We will communicate any such provisions via email and app notifications.

10.2 Long-term Rentals: For rentals exceeding 30 days, different refund terms may apply. Please contact us for details on long-term rental cancellation policies.

11. Insurance Refunds

Optional insurance purchased with your rental may have different refund terms:

  • Insurance is refundable if cancelled at least 48 hours before pickup
  • Insurance fees are non-refundable once the rental period begins
  • Unused insurance on early returns is not refundable

12. Gift Cards and Promotional Credits

Bookings made with gift cards or promotional credits:

  • Refunds will be issued back to the gift card or as account credit
  • Promotional credits used for bookings are not refundable in cash
  • Expired gift cards or credits cannot be reinstated

13. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  • Log into your My Rental App account
  • Go to "My Bookings" and select the relevant booking
  • Click "Cancel Booking" or "Request Refund"
  • Provide a reason for the cancellation/refund request
  • Submit any required documentation

Alternatively, contact our customer support team at refunds@myrentalapp.com or call +1 (555) 123-4567.
